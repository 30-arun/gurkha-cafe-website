Our Story

A warm welcome, Gurkha style

Think fragrant meat curries, hand-folded momo, crispy samosas, fried noodles and a generous range of vegetarian dishes — all made fresh to order.

We opened our doors in February 2026 on the former Village Bakery site in Harestock, and we've loved keeping that same community spirit alive while bringing a taste of Nepal to Winchester. Our team is always happy to help with allergies and talk you through the menu if it's your first time trying Nepali food.
